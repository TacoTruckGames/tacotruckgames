How it plays

Every Run Is a Descent

Fast, readable action with a build that comes together one blessing at a time, and a camp that remembers what you brought back.

A room ends by leaving it

Killing everything is not the objective. A keybearer stirs, drops the key, and the gate opens: walk through it while the siege keeps coming.

Blessings of three gods

Odin, Thor and Loki each offer their favour. Stack a blessing three deep, or hold two gods at once and evolve them into something neither meant.

Burn, frost, shock

Every hit can carry a status, and statuses react. Freeze what is burning, shock what is frozen, and watch the room come apart.

One skill, one ultimate

No hotbar to manage. Each hero has an attack, a skill on a short cooldown, and an ultimate charged by the damage you deal.

The camp remembers

Gear drops, and comes home with you. Wear it, forge it, spend on talents that last for good, and take bounties back down.

Eight lords, eight realms

Each realm ends at its lord, with a telegraphed fight that rewards reading the ground. Kill one and the stair under its seat keeps going down.
