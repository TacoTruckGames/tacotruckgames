Frequently Asked Questions

When is Depths of Ruin coming out?

Depths of Ruin is coming to Steam. We'll announce the release date, and a playable demo, on this page and on the Steam store page once it's live.

Is it free-to-play?

No. The Steam release is a premium game: one price, no ads, no in-app purchases, no energy timers.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Depths of Ruin is fully playable offline.

Where is my save?

Your progress is saved locally on your computer. Uninstalling the game may remove it, so back it up if you intend to reinstall.

The game is crashing or not loading.

Please try the following:

  • Make sure your graphics drivers are up to date.
  • Verify the game files through Steam (right-click the game, Properties, Installed Files, Verify).
  • Restart the game, then your computer.

If the issue persists, please contact us with details about your system and the problem.
