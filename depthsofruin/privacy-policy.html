Applicability

This privacy policy applies to Depths of Ruin on all platforms where it is distributed, including Steam. References to "this Application" throughout this document refer to the Game on any supported platform.

Data Collection

This Application does not collect, store, or transmit any Personal Data to the Owner. The Application does not use advertising, and it does not use any third-party analytics or crash-reporting services that collect user data.

All game progress and settings are stored locally on the User's device. The Application may keep a local log of gameplay events (such as runs started and completed) on the User's device for the purpose of improving the game; this log is not transmitted to the Owner or any third party.

No Tracking

This Application does not track Users. It does not use cookies, unique device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any other tracking technologies. The Application does not share data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Third-Party Services

On the Steam platform, the Application integrates with Steamworks for features such as achievements, cloud saves, and leaderboards. Steamworks is operated by Valve Corporation and is subject to Valve's own privacy policy. Any data handled by Steamworks (such as your Steam display name, achievements, and cloud-synced save files) is handled by Valve under that policy, not by the Owner.

Children's Privacy

This Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. Because this Application does not collect any Personal Data, no data about children is collected, stored, or transmitted.

The Rights of Users

Because this Application does not collect Personal Data, there is no user data held by the Owner to access, modify, or delete. If you have any questions or concerns about your privacy, you may contact the Owner at depthsofruin@tacotruckgames.com.

Account and Data Deletion

This Application does not create user accounts and does not collect or store Personal Data on any server. All game data is stored locally on the User's device (and, where enabled, in the User's own Steam Cloud storage) and can be removed by uninstalling the Application and deleting its save data.

If you have any questions about data deletion, you may contact the Owner at depthsofruin@tacotruckgames.com.
