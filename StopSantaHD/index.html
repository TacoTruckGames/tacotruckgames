What's new

More Than a Coat of Paint

The original was nine levels and three towers. The remaster keeps the joke and builds a real tower defense game around it.

60 levels, six regions

Nine became sixty, tuned onto one smooth difficulty curve from the first elf to the last night.

18 enemies, six bosses

Frost imps, tundra brutes, gumdrop golems and tin soldiers join the elves, penguins and zombie Santas.

Three free abilities

Blizzard, Candy Bomb and the Frost Totem, on cooldowns. Never consumables, never sold.

The star board

Stars you earn are a budget to spend on six upgrade tracks, and respec is always free.

Endless mode

Finish a region and run its finale lap after lap, each one meaner than the last. Best lap kept.

A story in 36 panels

An intro and an ending for every region, told straight-faced over a very silly war.
