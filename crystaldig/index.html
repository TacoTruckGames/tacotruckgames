A Block Puzzle Adventure

Place blocks, clear rows and columns, and collect sparkling gems. Then take the dig across the solar system — 400 levels, 8 worlds, 40 bosses. Free to play.

See It in Action

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Drag blocks onto the board and fill a full row or column to clear it. Gems buried in the blocks drop into your pouch, and chaining several clears at once builds big combos.

Out of room? A Pickaxe, Shuffle or Undo can save the run.

Some screenshots and the trailer show seasonal themes that arrive in the next update.

Two Ways to Play

Classic Mode

Endless block puzzle. Place blocks, clear rows and columns, and chase your best score. Simple to learn, endlessly challenging — and your high score goes up on the Game Center and Google Play Games leaderboards.

Adventure Mode

Journey across the solar system through 400 handcrafted levels. Dig down through 40 underground layers, starting on Earth and travelling on to Mars, Venus, Mercury, Europa, Titan, Miranda and Triton — with a boss battle waiting at the bottom of every layer.

Eight Worlds

Eight worlds, five underground layers each, 50 levels per world. Every layer has its own look, hazards and secrets — and a guardian waiting at the bottom.
