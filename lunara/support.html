Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my zodiac sign?

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner to open Settings. Under 'Zodiac Sign,' select your sign from the picker.

My horoscope isn't loading.

Lunara includes pre-loaded content covering March 2026 through March 2027. If a reading isn't available for a specific date, it may be outside the current content range. Please try restarting the app. If the issue persists, contact us.

How accurate are the birth charts?

Lunara uses the Swiss Ephemeris engine — the same astronomical data source used by professional astrologers worldwide. Charts use the Placidus house system, the most common in Western astrology. For the most accurate results, include your exact birth time. Without birth time, house positions and rising sign will be approximate.

Is my birth data private?

Yes. Your birth date, time, and location are stored exclusively on your device and are never sent to our servers or any third party. You can delete your birth chart data at any time by tapping 'Recalculate' on the Chart tab.

How do I set up daily notifications?

Open Settings (gear icon) and toggle 'Daily Horoscope' on. You can also set your preferred delivery time using the time picker below the toggle.

The app is crashing or not loading.

Please try the following:

  • Make sure your device is running the latest version of iOS.
  • Make sure you have the latest version of Lunara installed.
  • Restart the app by closing it completely and reopening it.
  • Restart your device.

If the issue persists, please contact us with details about your device and the problem.
