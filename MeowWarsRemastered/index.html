How a fight works

Three lanes, a hand of cats and tricks, and one big button.

  1. Play your cats

    Spend goldfish to drop cats into three lanes and play tricks like Hairball and Catnip.

  2. Read the board

    Every heart shows the health it will have left, so you know the result before you commit.

  3. Press Fight!

    Each cat hits what stands across from it. An empty lane means the hit lands on the commander.

Mission briefing

Same cats, new game.

  • Roguelite runs

    Fight your way across the island: scouts, bosses, and a rest stop where you heal or train a card.

  • Deckbuilding

    Start with ten cards. Add one after every fight and collect cat toys that last the whole run.

  • Online versus

    Create a room and duel a friend in real time, or jump into a Quick Battle against any boss.

  • Hand-inked look

    Every card, commander and menu redrawn in marker and ink on paper.

  • Achievements and leaderboards

    33 achievements to earn, plus leaderboards on Game Center and Play Games.

  • Made for phones and tablets

    Built for touch from the first screen, in 11 languages.
