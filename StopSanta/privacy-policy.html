Privacy Policy

Stop Santa collects a small amount of usage data to help us improve the game. No ads, no accounts, and you can turn it off.

The Short Version

  • The game sends us usage data: which levels are played, won and lost, how long sessions last, and which in-app purchases are made.
  • That data is tied to a random identifier the game creates for itself. It is not tied to your name, your email address or any account.
  • We run the analytics service ourselves. The data is not sold and is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The game shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can turn collection off at any time with the Share anonymous data switch in the game's Settings.

Taco Truck Games, LLC

Applicability

This privacy policy applies to Stop Santa: Tower Defense ("this Application") version 2.0 and later, on every platform where it is distributed, including the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Versions before 2.0, including the original release on Steam, do not contain the analytics described here and do not send usage data to the Owner.

What This Application Collects

While the Share anonymous data switch is on, this Application sends the following to the Owner's own analytics service at telemetry.tacotruckgames.com. The switch is on by default.

DataWhat it is
Random install identifier A random identifier this Application creates for itself on your device. It is not your device's advertising identifier, it is not taken from your hardware, and it is not shared with any other app. Deleting this Application removes it from your device.
Gameplay activity When a play session starts and ends and how long it lasted. Which levels and regions you start, win or lose, the stars you earn, how far you have reached in the campaign, and your Endless mode results.
Purchases When you buy an in-app purchase: which item, its price and the currency. Payment is handled by Apple or Google. The Owner never receives your card details, billing address or store account.
Device and app information Operating system and its version, device model, device type (phone, tablet or desktop), the language and region setting of your device, and the version of this Application.
Country The country your connection comes from, worked out from your IP address when the data arrives. Your IP address itself is not saved in the analytics records. It is used only at that moment to find the country and to limit abusive traffic.

What This Application Does Not Collect

This Application does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifier. It does not create user accounts. It shows no advertising and contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.

How the Data Is Used

The Owner uses this data only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. For example:

  • to find levels that are too hard, too easy, or where players stop playing;
  • to see which versions of the game and which kinds of devices are in use;
  • to count how many people play and how often;
  • to see which in-app purchases are bought.

The data is not used for advertising, is not used to build a profile of you, and is not combined with data from other companies.

Your Choice

Open Settings in the game and turn off Share anonymous data. From that moment this Application stops sending usage data and discards anything it had not yet sent. You can turn the switch back on at any time. The game plays the same either way.

Who Receives the Data

The data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to an analytics service operated by the Owner. That service runs on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes the data on the Owner's behalf as a service provider.

The Owner does not sell this data and does not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, except where required by law.

How Long the Data Is Kept

Individual event records are deleted 90 days after they are received.

Summary records are kept for as long as the Owner operates analytics for this Application. These are: one record per install (first and last day seen, game version, country, language setting, operating system, device model and type), the days on which an install was active, play sessions and their length, and purchases.

Platform Services

This Application relies on services run by the store you got it from. Those companies act under their own privacy policies.

  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The store tells this Application what you own so it can unlock the content.
  • Achievements and leaderboards. Versions of this Application that offer achievements and leaderboards use Apple Game Center on Apple devices and Google Play Games Services on Android. If you are signed in to one of those services, this Application sends your achievement progress and leaderboard scores to it, and your scores appear on leaderboards under the name you use on that service. The Owner does not receive your name, email address or account details from these services. This Application keeps the player identifier supplied by the service on your device only, to remember what has already been sent.
  • Steam. The Steam release integrates with Steamworks, operated by Valve Corporation, for features such as achievements.

Children's Privacy

This Application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Owner does not knowingly collect Personal Data from children. The usage data described above does not include a name, contact details or any account. If you believe a child has provided Personal Data to the Owner, contact stopsanta@tacotruckgames.com.

Your Rights and Data Deletion

This Application does not create user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

The usage data is tied to a random identifier and not to your name, email address or any account. For that reason the Owner is generally not able to tell which records came from you, and may not be able to find, export, correct or delete the records of one specific person.

What you can do:

  • Turn off Share anonymous data in Settings to stop all further collection.
  • Delete this Application to remove the random identifier, your progress and your settings from your device.
  • Individual event records are deleted automatically after 90 days.

If you have a question or a request about your data, write to stopsanta@tacotruckgames.com and the Owner will help as far as it is able to.
